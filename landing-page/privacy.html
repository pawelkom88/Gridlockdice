Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

GridlockDice does not collect any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising framework, and no third-party tracking of any kind. This policy explains exactly what data the app does and does not handle.

1. Data Collection

GridlockDice collects nothing. The app contains no analytics library, no telemetry, no crash-reporting framework, and no advertising SDK. It is built using only Apple's first-party frameworks (SwiftUI and SpriteKit). No data of any kind is transmitted from your device to us or to any third party.

2. App Store Purchases

When you purchase the full game through the App Store, payment processing is handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit. GridlockDice never receives or stores your payment information. Apple may share a receipt token with the app to confirm your purchase status, but this token is processed on-device and is not transmitted anywhere else.

3. No Account Required

GridlockDice does not require an account, registration, email address, or any other personal information to play. There is no sign-up screen, no login prompt, and no profile system anywhere in the app.

4. No Internet Requirement

The entire game runs offline. No internet connection is required to play any of the 50 levels. The app does not phone home, check for license validation, serve ads, or download additional content.

5. No Push Notifications

GridlockDice does not send push notifications. The app has no remote notification capability and will never request notification permissions.

6. No Third-Party Code

The app binary contains zero third-party SDKs, libraries, or frameworks. All code is written using Apple-provided APIs. There are no embedded trackers, no monetisation SDKs, and no external service integrations.

7. Data Storage

Game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in persistence mechanisms (UserDefaults and FileManager). This data never leaves your device and is automatically removed when you delete the app.

8. Children's Privacy

GridlockDice does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, no parental consent is required. The app contains no user-generated content, no messaging, no social features, and no external links.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the new date. Since the app receives no data, any policy change would only affect how we describe what the app already does.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please create an issue on the GridlockDice GitHub repository or contact the developer through the App Store support channel.
