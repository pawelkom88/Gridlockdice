Read the grid

The board appears labelled A–F down and 1–6 across. White dice mark fixed coordinate positions. Reading that space — understanding what the dice block and what they leave open — is the entire game.

Drag the pieces

Colourful polyomino blocks sit in a scrollable tray below the grid. Drag them into position. A green glow confirms a valid fit; red means it won't go. Haptics respond to every placement, error, and solve.

Cover every empty cell and the puzzle resolves — checkmark, star rating, your time. A progress bar tracks your path from level 1 to 50. No timer pressure, but the clock is always visible.

Features

Everything included.

One purchase, no gating. Every feature and every level unlocked permanently.

Dice as obstacles

Fixed white dice mark specific coordinates — A1, C3, F5 — and sit immovably in the grid. They replace generic blocked-cell patterns with spatial logic grounded in the coordinate system.

Guaranteed solvable

Levels 6–50 are procedurally generated, but every one is verified by a backtracking solver before it ships. If it's in the game, at least one valid solution exists. No unsolvable puzzles, ever.

Piece rotation

From level 21, tap a piece in the tray to rotate it 90°. Four orientations, one touch. It layers geometric depth into the core mechanic without changing how the game fundamentally works.

Hint & auto-solve

The hint engine runs the built-in solver in real time and highlights the next best placement. Auto-solve completes any level instantly with animation. Both are always available — no strings attached.

Native iOS craft

Built entirely in SwiftUI and SpriteKit. Spring animations, precise haptic feedback, full VoiceOver support, reduced motion, and dynamic sizing across every iPhone and iPad model.

Zero third-party code

No SDKs, no trackers, no analytics. Pure Apple frameworks only. 127 automated tests ship with every release. The binary is lean, behaviour is predictable, and your data never leaves your device.
